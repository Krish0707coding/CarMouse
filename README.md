SPEEDY MOUSE

This is a fully custom, functional mouse in the shape of a sports car that I designed and will be 3D printed to hold all the hardware parts, which I tried to make sleek like a sports car. The navigation is as speedy as the car! Built for Macondo, Hack Club. 

REQUIRED PARTS:

 - Main PCB board
 - Wireless USB reviever
 - Scroll wheel
 - Optical Lens
 - Battery Lens
 - On/Off Toggle switch
 - Mounting screws and Adhesive sliding pads (skates)
 - AA batteries
 - 3D Printed car shaped mouse design I designed

ASSEMBLY INSTRUCTIONS:

 1. Remove all supports from the newly 3d printed car cover and clean out any internal mounting grooves
 2. Place the power switch in its designated spot
 3. Place the optical lens in the center opening with the flat side down.
 4. Slide the negative and positive battery terminals in their respected spots
 5. Tuck the wires into the side
 6. Place the scroll wheel onto the metal bracket on the mainboard PCB
 7. Lower the mainboard into the bottom shell
 8. Plug the battery connector into the matching white socket.
 9. Screw the mainboard down using 3 screws
 10. Hover the top shell over the bottom base
 11. use two screws to secure the top shell to the bottom shell
 12. Apply the 4 skates, and insert the AA Battery
